Biology Blurting Technique

  1. Pick 1 Specification Bullet: e.g. Glycolysis & Link Reaction.
  2. Blurt (5 Mins): Write out every enzyme, substrate, ATP yield, and location from pure memory on a blank sheet.
  3. Green Pen Gap Fill: Open textbook/notes. Add missed details in green pen. Focus on exact bolded mark scheme terms.
  4. Convert Gaps to Anki: Immediately turn your green-pen gaps into flashcards for low-energy recall.

Chemistry Worked Calculations

  • Always write state symbols and units in intermediate working out.
  • Mechanism Rules: Curly arrows MUST start precisely at a lone pair or electron-rich bond and point directly to an electron-deficient atom.
  • Physical Chemistry (pH, Ka, Kp): Express numbers to the correct significant figures (usually 3 s.f. in A-Level Chem).
  • Error Log Rule: If you lose marks on a unit conversion (e.g. $J \rightarrow kJ$ or $cm^3 \rightarrow dm^3$), write it in the error log immediately.

OCR Psych GRAVE Evaluation

Use this framework for 10 & 15 mark evaluation essays across all 20 Core Studies:

G - Generalizability: Sample size, andro/gynocentric, ethos.
R - Reliability: Standardization, internal consistency, replicate test.
A - Application / Utility: Real-world usefulness in schools/prisons/hospitals.
V - Validity: Construct, ecological, demand characteristics.
E - Ethics: Deception, right to withdraw, protection from harm.
